Baking soda is the reason most natural deodorants work: it raises the pH under the arm so odor bacteria cannot thrive. It is also mildly alkaline against skin that is slightly acidic, and for some people that is enough to cause a rash, especially right after shaving.

How to tell

  • Itching, pink patches or a burning feeling within the first few days: baking soda.
  • Smell by afternoon without irritation: the adjustment period, keep going.
  • Fine for months, then a sudden rash: often shaving plus a thick application. Try less product and a day off after shaving before deciding.

What we use instead

Bentonite clay. It absorbs moisture and odor compounds without changing pH. The Baking Soda Free stick keeps coconut oil, shea butter, arrowroot, diatomaceous earth and beeswax, drops the baking soda, adds bentonite, and uses the same essential oil blends: the Original or Cedarwood Lavender.
