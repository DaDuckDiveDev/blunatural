Blakbird exists because of one conversation in our kitchen: "I'll use it if it doesn't smell like a candle." So we kept the formula and changed the scent.

No.1

Essential oils of cedarwood, sage, tea tree and vanilla. Woody, a little sweet, closer to aftershave than to a spa. Contains baking soda, so it is the stronger of the two.

No.3

No essential oils, no baking soda. An infusion of organic sandalwood and local vanilla bean in the coconut oil. Softer scent, kinder to skin that reacts to either. It is our second-bestselling stick overall, and about half the people buying it are not men.

The soap

The Blakbird bar is activated charcoal, tea tree, orange and bergamot in a certified organic base with cocoa and shea butter. Same line, same idea.
