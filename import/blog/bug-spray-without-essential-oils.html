Mosquitoes find you by smell. Most natural repellents work by covering that smell with essential oils strong enough to bother the mosquito, which can also bother your skin. We went the slow way.

The infusion

Witch hazel, which is already mildly astringent and soothing on bites, is steeped for two to three weeks with organic peppermint leaf, organic lavender flowers and vanilla beans grown on Oʻahu's North Shore. The vanilla is not just for the smell, though it is why this is the one bug spray people put on on purpose.

From the archive: the vanilla bean sourcing post and the infusion jars.

How to use it

  • Shake, spray exposed skin, reapply every couple of hours or after swimming.
  • Fine on children's skin; keep it out of eyes.
  • 2 oz fits a hiking pack. It is a repellent, not a treatment for bites, though the witch hazel helps with the itch.
