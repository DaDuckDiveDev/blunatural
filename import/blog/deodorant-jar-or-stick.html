People ask which one works better. They are the same recipe. The difference is the container and how you put it on.

Pick the jar if

  • You want less plastic: glass jar, metal lid, wooden spatula.
  • You live somewhere hot. A soft jar is still a jar.
  • You like control over how much you use. A pea-sized amount is a full application.

Pick the stick if

  • You want it fast and dry-handed.
  • You travel with it in a bag.
  • You are buying for someone who will not use a spatula.

How to apply the jar

Scoop a pea-sized amount with the spatula or a clean fingertip. Press it against the underarm for a second so it melts, then spread. It feels like a balm for ten seconds and then disappears. Wash the spatula once a week.
