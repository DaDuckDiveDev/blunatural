Essential oils are concentrated: a drop of peppermint oil is hundreds of leaves. That is why they work in deodorant, and why some skin says no. The alternative is older than essential oils: put the plant itself in the oil and wait.

How the infusion works

Organic coconut oil is warmed gently with dried peppermint leaf, or with hibiscus, chamomile and calendula, and held there for weeks. The oil takes on the plant's compounds slowly. The scent is noticeably lighter than the essential-oil sticks and sits closer to the skin; people around you will not smell it, you will.

Who this is for

  • Anyone who gets a reaction from essential oils.
  • Pregnancy, when many people avoid essential oils as a precaution.
  • Teenagers starting with natural deodorant.
  • People who just want it quiet.

The two infusions

  • Peppermint Leaf: cool and green.
  • Hibiscus Chamomile Calendula: soft and floral, the gentlest stick we make.
