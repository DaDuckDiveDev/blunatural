The whole ingredient list fits on the side of the tube. Here is the long version.

Coconut oil

The base. Mildly antibacterial on its own, melts at skin temperature so it spreads, and it is why the stick softens in heat.

Organic arrowroot powder

Absorbs moisture. The reason you feel dry without an antiperspirant.

Baking soda (aluminum-free)

Raises pH so odor bacteria cannot thrive. The active ingredient, and the one that irritates some skin; the Baking Soda Free stick swaps it for bentonite clay.

Organic diatomaceous earth

Fossilized algae, ground fine. Absorbent and gently deodorizing. Food grade.

White kaolin clay

The softest clay. Absorbs oil without drying skin, gives the stick its smooth glide.

Organic beeswax

Holds the stick together above coconut oil's melting point and leaves a thin breathable layer.

Organic shea butter

Conditions the skin so daily use does not dry it out. Also softens the feel of the baking soda.

Essential oils

Scent, and most of them (tea tree especially) are antibacterial. The Original is tea tree, eucalyptus, peppermint and rosemary. Skip them with the Essential Oil Free or Unscented sticks.

What is not in it

Aluminum, parabens, propylene glycol, synthetic fragrance, talc, water. Not because we are afraid of chemistry; everything is chemistry. Because none of them are needed for the job.
