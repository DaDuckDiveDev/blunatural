A bar of soap is oil and lye and time. Here is what that looks like at our table.

The oils

Sunflower, coconut, palm and safflower, all certified organic. Saponified, they become soap plus glycerin, and we keep the glycerin in the bar; commercial soap often removes it to sell separately, which is why it can feel stripping.

Scent and color

Essential oils and botanicals do both: annatto seed turns the citrus bar orange, activated charcoal makes the Blakbird bar black, oats and honey speckle the oatmeal bar. Nothing synthetic.

The cure

After the pour, bars rest four to six weeks before they are hard enough to last in the shower. Then each one is cut, stamped with the monstera leaf, and boxed in recycled card.
