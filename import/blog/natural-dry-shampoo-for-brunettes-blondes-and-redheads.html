Arrowroot powder absorbs oil beautifully and is pure white, which is why most natural dry shampoo looks fine on blonde hair and like dust on brown. The fix is a pigment that is also absorbent: clay.

Three shades, four ingredients

  • Blonde: organic arrowroot, organic ground cinnamon, Brazilian yellow clay.
  • Red: organic arrowroot, organic ground cinnamon, Moroccan red clay.
  • Brunette: organic arrowroot, organic ground cinnamon, rhassoul clay, cocoa powder.

Cinnamon keeps the powder fresh and adds a faint warmth to the scent; there is no fragrance.

How to apply without the cloud

Sprinkle a little into your palm, rub your fingertips in it, and work it into the roots where hair is oiliest. For a lighter touch, tap a powder brush into a small bowl of it and dust the part line. Wait a minute, then brush through. Less is more; it is doing the job even when you cannot see it.
