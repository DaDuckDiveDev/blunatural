Every summer we get the same email: "my deodorant arrived melted." It is not ruined. Here is the chemistry and the fix.

The chemistry

Coconut oil is solid below about 76°F and liquid above it. Beeswax and shea butter raise the melting point of the blend, but a mail truck in July runs well past 100°F. The stick goes soft, sometimes separates a little, and then re-solidifies when it cools. Nothing in it spoils; there is no water in the formula for anything to grow in.

The fix

  • Stand the tube upright, cap on, in the fridge for an hour.
  • Take it out, let it sit ten minutes, use as normal.
  • If it separated, you may see a lighter layer on top for the first use. It evens out.

Storing it

Room temperature, out of direct sun. Not the car, not a windowsill. In a Hawaiʻi or Texas summer without air conditioning, the fridge is fine as a home. The Jar version does not care about softening at all, which is one reason to pick it.
