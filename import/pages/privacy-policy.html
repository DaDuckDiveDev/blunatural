Blu Naturals ("we") runs blunaturals.com. This policy explains what we collect when you use the site, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Order information: name, shipping and billing address, email, phone, and what you bought. We need this to fulfil and support your order. Payment card details go directly to our payment processor and never touch our server.
  • Account information if you create an account: your email and order history.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: your email and where on the site you signed up.
  • Site usage: standard server logs and, if enabled, privacy-respecting analytics (page views, referrer, device type). We do not sell or share this.

Who we share it with

Only the services needed to run the shop: our payment processor (for payment), USPS (for delivery), our hosting provider, and our email service (for order emails and, if you opted in, the newsletter). Each is bound by its own privacy terms. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context advertising.

Cookies

The site uses cookies that make the cart and checkout work, and a cookie that remembers if you dismissed the newsletter prompt. Analytics, if enabled, runs without cross-site tracking cookies.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter with the link in any email.
  • Ask us to export or delete your data from the contact page. We will confirm within 30 days.
  • California, Colorado, Virginia and other state residents: see Do not sell or share my personal information.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Contact

Blu Naturals, Honolulu, Hawaiʻi. Use the contact page.
