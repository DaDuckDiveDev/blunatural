Shipping

Everything ships from Honolulu, Hawaiʻi by USPS, usually within two business days of your order. Mainland delivery is typically 3 to 5 business days after that; neighbor islands 1 to 2.

  • Orders of $40 and over: free US shipping.
  • Orders under $40: $7 flat.
  • US addresses only for now, including Hawaiʻi, Alaska, Puerto Rico and APO/FPO.

Deodorant is mostly coconut oil and can soften in a hot mailbox. It is not damaged; stand it upright in the fridge for an hour before first use. In summer we pack sticks with a paper sleeve, but we cannot control the temperature of a mail truck in Texas.

Returns and the two-week promise

Natural deodorant takes about two weeks to settle in. If after two weeks of daily use it is not working for you, email us with your order number and we will either send a different formula (usually the baking-soda-free or unscented stick) or refund the product. No need to send anything back; a used stick is not something we can resell.

Unopened products can be returned within 30 days for a refund of the product price. Soap, dry shampoo and bug spray follow the same rule. Digital downloads and gift cards are not refundable once delivered.

Damaged in transit? Send a photo within 7 days of delivery and we will replace it.
